**Posts Statistics** shows how your Substack newsletter posts perform: subscriber counts and ARR, email open and delivery rates, interactions, and a ranked table of your posts. The numbers come from Substack and are collected by the WriteStack browser extension.

## Before you start

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | Standard or Enterprise. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits). |
| Extension | [WriteStack extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) version 1.5.25 or newer |
| Substack | A connected Substack account with at least one newsletter |

## Steps

1. Open WriteStack with the extension installed. WriteStack fetches post stats on its own for every newsletter you own on Substack (as owner or admin), about once a day. You don't need to pick them first.
2. Keep the tab open while the first fetch runs. Posts Statistics shows "Fetching your posts" meanwhile. It can take a few minutes if you have hundreds of posts.
3. Open **Posts Statistics** in the sidebar.
4. Use the bar under the page title to filter: **Newsletter** (default **All newsletters**; shown when you have two or more), **Date range**, **Compare to** and **Chart interval**. See [Date range, compare and interval](#date-range-compare-and-interval).

To change which newsletters are included, click **Choose newsletters**. Untick a newsletter to leave it out, or tick one you write for but don't own. A newsletter you start owning later is included on its own and shows up there marked **New**. Use **Refresh newsletters** if one is missing.

## What the page shows

### Subscribers

Live counts from Substack's own subscriber chart, not sums of post conversions. Switch the metric between **Free subscribers**, **Paid subscribers** and **ARR**. The section also shows **Paid now**, **Total now**, and the change across the selected range. Paid subscribers are split into Paid, Free trials, Gifts and Comps.

#### Your posts on the chart

A strip of small cover images under the Subscribers chart marks the day each post went out, so you can see which posts came before a jump in subscribers.

- Hover a cover to see the post's title and date. Click a title to open the post on Substack.
- In the Weekly, Monthly and Yearly views, and when posts are too close together to fit, one cover stands for several posts and shows how many. Hover it to see them all.
- Hover the chart on a date to see that day's posts in the tooltip.

### How posts land

| Tile | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open rate** | Substack's unique email opens divided by emails delivered |
| **Email traffic** | The share of all views that came from email, including repeat views |
| **Engagement rate** | Average across emailed posts |
| **Delivery rate** | Emails delivered out of emails sent |

<Callout type="info">
Open rate and email traffic are different numbers. Open rate counts each reader once. Email traffic is about views, so one reader opening an email three times adds three views. Use **Open rate** when you compare with Substack's dashboard.
</Callout>

### Interaction mix

Totals for posts published in the range: **Views**, **Likes**, **Comments**, **Restacks** and **Shares**.

### Post type and Audience

- **Post type** splits views by the kind of post.
- **Audience** splits views by the Substack audience setting of each post (who can read it).

### Top posts

A table of your posts with **Views**, **Unique opens**, **Open rate**, **Clicks**, **Free subs** and **Paid subs**. Click a column header to rank posts by it. Click a title to read the post on Substack.

## Date range, compare and interval

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Today, Yesterday, Last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days (default), Last 3 months, Last 6 months, Last 12 months, Week to date, Month to date, Year to date, All time, Custom |
| **Compare to** | Previous period (default), Previous month, Previous year, Custom |
| **Chart interval** | Daily (default), Weekly, Monthly, Yearly |

With a comparison on, the Subscribers chart draws the earlier period as a dashed line, the legend names both date ranges, and the change line reads "across this range, vs" the change in the earlier period. The comparison works the same way as on the Notes Statistics page: see [Compare with an earlier period](/docs/using/analytics/notes-analytics#compare-with-an-earlier-period). To turn it off, click the **x** at the left of **Compare to**. **All time** has no earlier period, so it can't be compared.

You can add an annotation to a point on the Subscribers chart, for example the day you launched a paid tier: hover a point and click the note icon.

## How often it updates

The extension fetches post stats when you open WriteStack, at most once a day. The page header shows **Last updated** with the time of the last fetch.

To fetch now instead of waiting, click **Update now** next to **Last updated**. It fetches your post and subscriber stats from Substack, shows the fetching banner while it runs, and refreshes the page when it's done. It needs the [WriteStack extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) and a Substack sign-in in the same browser. If an update is already running, WriteStack tells you "Your stats are already updating."

## If it doesn't work

- **"No newsletters of yours yet"**: none of the newsletters on your Substack profile are yours, or you unticked them all. Click **Choose newsletters** and tick the ones to include.
- **"No newsletters found"**: WriteStack found no newsletter on your Substack profile. Connect a publication in Settings, then open the page again.
- **"No post stats yet"**: the first fetch hasn't run. Open WriteStack with the extension installed and keep the tab open until it finishes.
- **Numbers look out of date**: click **Update now**. Check that your extension is 1.5.25 or newer. See [Browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension).
- **"Couldn't update your stats"**: the extension isn't installed, or you aren't signed in to Substack in this browser.
- **"Posts Statistics isn't on for your account"**: your plan doesn't include it. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits).

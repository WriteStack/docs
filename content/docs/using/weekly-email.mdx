---
title: Weekly email
description: What's in WriteStack's Weekly Update email (Notes stats, note of the week, posting streak, and Posts stats on Standard and Enterprise), when it is sent, why a week can be skipped, and how to turn it off in Settings → Notifications.
---

Once a week WriteStack emails you how last week went on Substack: how your Notes did, your note of the week and your posting streak, plus your posts and subscribers on Standard and Enterprise. Turn it off any time in **Settings → Notifications → Weekly Update**.

## What's in it

The email covers last week, Monday to Sunday (UTC). It has up to two sections. Each section shows **Last updated** with the time WriteStack last fetched those stats.

### Notes

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Headline numbers | **Impressions**, **New subscribers** and **Notes posted**, each with the change from the week before |
| Chart | Impressions by the day each Note was posted (or Notes posted each day) |
| **Your note of the week** | The Note seen most last week (or most liked, if WriteStack has no impressions for you), plus the next best ones under **Also did well** |
| **The details** | Likes, replies, restacks, link clicks and more, with new paid subscribers and new yearly revenue when there were any |
| **Who saw your notes** | How much of your reach came from new people, followers and subscribers |
| Streak and rhythm | Your posting streak, how many days you posted, how many Notes are scheduled for this week, and when your followers are most active |
| **See full Notes stats** | Opens [Notes analytics](/docs/using/analytics/notes-analytics) for that week |

If you posted no Notes last week but did the week before, you get a short **A quiet week** block with a **Schedule this week's notes** button instead. If you posted no Notes in either week, there is no Notes section.

The posting streak in the email counts **days in a row** with a Note, and shows from 2 days. It is not the same as the week streak in the sidebar. See [Streak](/docs/using/streak).

### Posts (Standard and Enterprise)

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Subscriber numbers | **Subscribers**, **Paying subscribers** and **Yearly revenue**, with the change this week |
| Post numbers | **Views**, **Open rate**, **New subscribers** and **Likes** for the posts you published |
| Your posts | Each post you published last week, with a **Read** link |
| **See full Posts stats** | Opens [Posts Statistics](/docs/using/analytics/posts-statistics) for that week |

If you published no post, the section shows your subscriber numbers and "No new posts went out last week."

The Posts section needs a plan with Posts Statistics and at least one newsletter marked as yours on the Posts Statistics page. Without them, the email doesn't mention posts. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits).

## When it's sent

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Days | Monday to Thursday |
| Time | Around 08:00 UTC |
| How often | At most one email a week |
| Who gets it | Writers with an active subscription who haven't turned it off |

You get it on the first of those days that your stats are fresh.

### Fresh stats

WriteStack only puts a section in the email if it fetched those stats from Substack in the last 36 hours. Stats are fetched by the [WriteStack extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) when you open WriteStack in a browser where you're signed in to Substack.

So, in practice:

- If you opened WriteStack over the weekend or on Monday morning, the email usually arrives on Monday.
- If not, it comes the first morning after you open WriteStack, up to Thursday.
- If your Notes stats are fresh but your Posts stats aren't, you get a Notes-only email. A second email doesn't follow when Posts catch up.
- If no section is fresh by Thursday, or there is nothing to show, there is no email that week.

## Turn it off

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Switch off **Weekly Update** ("Receive a weekly email with how your Notes and Posts did").

The change takes effect right away. You can also click **Turn off weekly updates** at the bottom of any weekly email. **Email settings** in the same place opens **Settings → Notifications**.

## If it doesn't work

- **No email this week**: open WriteStack on your computer with the extension installed and signed in to Substack. The next morning's run, up to Thursday, will include you.
- **No Posts section**: check that your plan includes [Posts Statistics](/docs/using/analytics/posts-statistics) and that you picked your newsletters there.
- **The numbers differ a little from the Statistics page**: the email counts Notes and posts published last week, with the totals they had collected when the stats were fetched. A Note posted on Sunday had less time to collect impressions than one posted on Monday.

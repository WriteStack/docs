Your streak is the number of weeks in a row in which you published at least one Note on Substack. It shows as a flame with a number next to the WriteStack logo at the top of the sidebar. Click it to open your streak card.

## How it counts

- A week runs Monday to Sunday, in your time zone.
- One published Note is enough for the week. More Notes don't make it longer.
- Your own Notes count. Restacking someone else's Note doesn't.
- The current week doesn't break the streak until it is over. If you haven't posted yet this week, the streak still shows the weeks up to last week.
- A week with no Note ends the streak. The badge disappears until you post again.

The streak is built from the Notes WriteStack has fetched from your Substack profile, so a Note you just posted may take a while to count.

## The streak card

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **N week streak** | Your current streak |
| Status | **Safe this week**, **On track** or **At risk** (see below) |
| Best | **Personal best** when this is your longest streak, otherwise **Best: N weeks** |
| This week | One dot per day, Monday to Sunday. A check means you posted that day. A dashed blue dot is a day with a scheduled Note. |
| Button | **Write a note**, or **Schedule a note** when the streak is at risk. It opens a new Note in the editor. |

### Status

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Safe this week** | You already posted this week. The streak is safe until Monday. |
| **On track** | You haven't posted yet, but a Note in your queue goes out later this week. The card shows when. |
| **At risk** | No Note this week and none scheduled. Post one before the week ends. The badge turns amber with a dot. |

**On track** counts Notes scheduled in your WriteStack queue. A scheduled Note only posts if it is synced to Substack. See [Sync](/docs/using/concepts/sync).

## Keep it alive

- Schedule at least one Note for every week. See [Manage the queue](/docs/using/schedule/queue).
- Turn on **Streak Reminder Notifications** in **Settings → Notifications** to get a daily email reminder.
- The [weekly email](/docs/using/weekly-email) shows your posting streak too. That one counts days in a row with a Note, not weeks.

**Inspirations** (in the sidebar) shows high-performing Substack notes and articles by other writers, picked to match what you write about. Use them as ideas for your own notes and posts, or send one to Quill, the Coach, to ask why it worked.

The page has two tabs: **Notes** and **Articles**. WriteStack remembers the last tab you used.

## How it works

- The first time you open the page, WriteStack reads your publication and notes to learn your topics. You see **Learning about you** while it works. This happens once and usually takes under a minute.
- Without a search, both tabs show a feed matched to your publication. The Articles tab leaves out your own publication's articles, unless you pick yourself with **Search writer...**.
- What you like, hide and set in **Train feed** shapes what you see next. See [Train the feed](#train-the-feed).
- You need a connected Substack publication. See [Connect your Substack](/docs/using/setup/connect-substack).

## Notes tab

Each card shows the note, its author, how long ago it was posted, and its likes, restacks and comments.

| On a note card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click the note text | Opens it in the note editor as a starting point for your own note. The editor shows "Inspired by" and the author's name. See [Write a Note](/docs/using/notes/drafting). |
| **Show more** | Shows the rest of a long note |
| View on Substack (arrow icon) | Opens the note on Substack in a new tab |
| Click the author's name | Opens a menu: **WriteStack profile**, **View creator on Substack**, **Hide creator's notes** |

More notes load as you scroll.

### Search notes

On Enterprise, a **Search notes** box sits at the top of the Notes tab.

1. Type a word or phrase in **Search notes**.
2. Press **Enter**, or click outside the box.
3. The page shows notes that contain your words, under "Notes about" and your search.

To go back to your feed, clear the box with the **X**.

### Sort and view

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Sort by** | **Relevance**, **Date**, **Most Liked**, **Most Commented**, **Most Restacked** |
| Grid or list | Switch between cards and one line per note |

Sort reorders the notes already on the page. When you filter by one writer, it sorts all of that writer's notes.

### Filters

On Enterprise, a filters panel sits on the right of the page.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last year**, or pick dates on the calendar |
| **Likes range** | At least 0, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1k, 2k or 5k likes |
| **Comments range** | At least 0, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 or 1k comments |
| **Reposts range** | At least 0, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 or 1k restacks |
| **Search writer...** | Shows notes by one writer. Clear it with the **X** next to their name. |

## Articles tab

The Articles tab finds top Substack articles (posts, not notes) on a topic. Each card shows the cover image, title, subtitle, author and publication, likes, comments, how long ago it was published, the reading time, and a **Paid** badge for paid articles. Click a card to read the article on Substack in a new tab.

### Find articles on a topic

1. Click the **Articles** tab.
2. Type a topic in **Search articles** and press **Enter**.
3. The page shows articles under "Articles about" and your topic. Search matches both meaning and exact words, in any language.

To go back to articles matched to your publication, clear the box with the **X**.

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Free or paid | **Free and paid** or **Free only** |
| Grid or list | Switch between cards and one row per article |
| Filters panel (Enterprise) | **Date range**, **Likes range**, **Comments range** and **Search writer...**, the same as on the Notes tab |

More articles load as you scroll.

## Card actions

Hover a note or article card to see three buttons in its top-right corner. On a phone or tablet they are always visible. In list view they sit in the row.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **More like this** (thumbs up) | Shows "We'll show you more like this". Future suggestions lean toward this kind of note or article. |
| **Less like this** (thumbs down) | Removes the card. That note or article stops showing up. |
| **Send to the Coach** (sparkles) | Opens **Ask Quill about this note** or **Ask Quill about this article** |

On an article, a thumbs down shows "We'll show you less like this" with **Undo** for a few seconds. Clicking the same thumb again on an article takes your rating back.

### Ask Quill about a note or article

1. Hover the card and click **Send to the Coach** (sparkles).
2. Type your question, or leave it empty to ask "Why did this work? Help me write my own, inspired by it."
3. Click **Send to Quill**, or press **Cmd+Enter** (**Ctrl+Enter** on Windows).
4. When "Quill is on it" appears, click **Open chat** to read the answer.

Each question starts a new Coach chat. For a note, Quill gets the note's text, author, likes, restacks and link. For an article, it gets the title, subtitle, author, likes, comments and link, and can open the article to read it.

The Coach can also search both tabs from a chat. See [What the Coach can do](/docs/using/coach/what-it-does).

## Train the feed

Click **Train feed** on the Notes tab to open **Train your feed**.

| Setting | What it does | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Muted keywords** | Hides notes that contain these words or phrases | Notes |
| **Preferred topics** | Topics you want more of | Notes and articles |
| **Prefer recent notes** | On by default. Your suggested notes come from the last 180 days, unless you pick a date range. Newer articles rank higher. | Notes and articles |
| **Hide near-duplicate posts** | Off by default. Filters out notes with very similar wording. | Notes |
| **Creator diversity** | **No limit**, **1 per creator**, **2 per creator** or **3 per creator** on each page of notes | Notes |
| **Hidden creators** | Writers you hid. Click **Unhide** to see them again. | Notes and articles |

Changes save as you make them. Close the dialog to refresh the feed. After you rate a note or hide a writer, a **Refresh feed** button appears next to **Train feed**.

### Hide a writer

1. On a note card, click the author's name.
2. Click **Hide creator's notes**.
3. Their notes leave the page, and a message such as "Jane's notes hidden" appears with **Undo** for a few seconds.

Hidden writers don't show up on the Articles tab either. To bring one back later, use **Unhide** under **Hidden creators** in **Train feed**.

## Limits

| | Hobbyist | Standard | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notes and Articles tabs | Included | Included | Included |
| Search articles, Free or paid | Included | Included | Included |
| Card actions and Send to the Coach | Included | Included | Included |
| **Search notes** box | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Filters panel (date, likes, comments, restacks, writer) | Not included | Not included | Included |
| **WriteStack profile** in the author menu | Not included | Included | Included |

The search box and filters are the "Advanced search through millions of Notes" line in [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits).

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Notes or articles per load | 20 |
| Articles per search | Up to about 400 |
| Your question to Quill | 2,000 characters |
| Note text sent to Quill | The first 3,000 characters |

- The filters panel only shows on a wide screen. On a narrow window or a phone, it's hidden.
- Quill gets an article's title and link, not its text. It opens the link when it needs to read the article.

## If it doesn't work

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Learning about you** stays on screen | Wait a minute. It only runs the first time. If it doesn't finish, reload the page. |
| Notes tab shows **Something went wrong** | Click **Try Again**. If it keeps happening, check that your Substack publication is connected. See [Connect your Substack](/docs/using/setup/connect-substack). |
| "Publication not found" on the Articles tab | Connect your Substack publication. See [Connect your Substack](/docs/using/setup/connect-substack). |
| "Your inspiration profile isn't ready yet" | Type a topic in **Search articles**, or add a topic under **Preferred topics** in **Train feed**. |
| **No notes found** | Change or clear your filters or search, then click **Try again** |
| "No articles match these filters yet." | Clear the search, set **Free and paid**, or loosen the filters |
| "Couldn't load more. Try again" | Click it to load the next articles again |
| No **Search notes** box or filters panel | They are on Enterprise only. On Enterprise, widen the window to see the filters panel. |
| A writer you want is missing | You may have hidden them. Check **Hidden creators** in **Train feed**. |
| An error in **Ask Quill about this note** | Read the message under the box. If you're out of Coach usage, see [Coach limits](/docs/using/coach/limits). |

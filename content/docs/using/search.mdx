---
title: Search with Cmd+K
description: Press Cmd+K on Mac, or Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux, anywhere in WriteStack to jump to a page or setting, switch to dark mode, start a note or article, or find your own notes, articles, Coach chats and published posts.
---

Press **Cmd+K** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, on any WriteStack page to open search. Type a few letters, use the arrow keys to pick a result, and press **Enter** to go there. Alt+K does not open search.

Search works from a computer keyboard only. There is no search button.

## What you can find

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Actions | **New note**, **New article**, **Ask Quill**, **Switch to dark mode** or **Switch to light mode**, **Contact support** |
| Pages | Every page in your sidebar, plus **Drafts**, **Scheduled notes**, **Published notes**, the Coach's **memory**, **library**, **skills**, **routines** and **apps**, and **What's new** |
| Settings | Every Settings page and the options inside them, such as **Password** or **Timezone**. Picking one opens that page and scrolls to the option. |
| Notes | Your notes whose text contains what you typed |
| Articles | Your articles whose title or subtitle contains what you typed |
| Coach chats | Your Coach chats whose title contains what you typed |
| Published posts | Your published Substack posts whose title or subtitle contains what you typed. These open on Substack in a new tab. |

Notes, articles, chats and posts show up after you type at least 2 characters. Each section shows up to 5 matches, newest first.

You only see pages and content you have access to on your plan. For example, the Articles section only appears if Articles is on for your account.

## Keys

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cmd+K on Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux | Open or close search |
| Up and down arrows | Move through results |
| Enter | Open the result |
| Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter) | Open the result in a new tab |
| Esc | Close search |

## If it doesn't work

- **Nothing opens.** Search doesn't open on top of another window, such as the note editor or a dialog. Close that first, then press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) again. Alt+K does not open search.
- **You wanted dark mode.** Choose **Switch to dark mode**, or use the profile menu or **Settings → Account**. See [Dark mode](/docs/using/appearance).
- **You pressed Cmd+K to add a link.** In the article editor, adding a link is **Cmd+Shift+K**. See [Article editor](/docs/using/articles/editor).
- **"Some of your content couldn't be searched right now."** One of your notes, articles, chats or posts lists didn't answer in time. Pages and settings still work. Try again in a moment.

Pushing sends a WriteStack article to Substack as a **draft**. It never publishes. After the first push, your edits go to the same draft on their own. To publish, schedule the article or publish it on Substack.

Every Substack action runs through the WriteStack browser extension in an open WriteStack tab. For how WriteStack and Substack stay in step in general, see [Sync](/docs/using/concepts/sync).

## What you need

- The [WriteStack browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension), version 1.5.28 or newer. Use 1.5.35 or newer to send audio and to update tags.
- You are logged into Substack in the same browser.
- WriteStack is open in a tab. Any WriteStack page works.

Editing Post settings, **Generate with AI** and **Disconnect from Substack** don't need the extension.

## Push a draft

1. Open the article on the **Articles** page.
2. Click **Push to Substack** at the top of the editor.

The first push creates the Substack draft. After that the button reads **Update Substack**, and **Open in Substack** opens the draft.

You can also push from an article's card or list row (**Push to Substack** or **Update on Substack**), or select several articles and push them together. Pushing several runs one at a time.

What a push does:

1. Saves the article.
2. Creates the Substack draft the first time.
3. Uploads images, audio and narration.
4. Writes the title, subtitle, body, thumbnail, [Post settings](/docs/using/articles/post-settings) and tags.

A push replaces the title, subtitle, body and settings of the Substack draft with the WriteStack version. If the Substack draft was edited after the last push, WriteStack stops with a [conflict](#resolve-a-conflict) instead.

**From the Coach:** ask, for example, "Push this to Substack." See [How the Coach writes articles](/docs/using/articles/coach-articles).

**From an AI agent (MCP):** the agent calls [`push_article_to_substack`](/docs/build/mcp-reference/push_article_to_substack) with the newsletter you chose. An article that already has a Substack draft stays on that newsletter.

## Edits are pushed on their own

Once an article is on Substack, your edits are pushed about 3 seconds after you stop typing. No button needed.

This stops when the article is:

- scheduled or published on Substack (click **Update Substack** or **Schedule** again to send edits),
- in conflict or blocked,
- a video or podcast post.

The status next to the save state tells you where things are:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not on Substack** | Not pushed yet. |
| **Updating Substack…** | A push is running. It says when images and audio are uploading. |
| **Up to date** | Substack has your latest version. |
| **Changes not on Substack** | You have edits Substack doesn't have yet. |
| **Needs attention** | A conflict, or the extension is blocked. Open **Settings → Publishing**. |

## Pull from Substack

**Pull from Substack** (in **Settings → Publishing**) replaces the WriteStack title, subtitle and body with the current Substack draft. Click **Pull from Substack** again to confirm. It also brings in the schedule from Substack.

The article must be pushed once first.

## Schedule on Substack

1. Open **Settings → Publishing**.
2. Under **Schedule**, pick a date and time.
3. Check the timezone. It starts as your computer's timezone. Click it to pick another.
4. Click the button. It shows how long until the post goes out, like **Schedule in 3 hours**.

Scheduling always pushes your latest edits too. Once scheduled, the card shows "Scheduled on Substack for" the date, and an **Unschedule** button.

- The timezone is only used for this post. Your account timezone stays the same.
- Changing the timezone keeps the same clock time. 6 PM stays 6 PM in the new timezone, so the post goes out at a different moment.
- A time that has already passed can't be scheduled.
- If you scheduled the post on Substack instead, WriteStack shows that time after the next push or pull.
- A scheduled article can't be moved on the calendar. Unschedule it first.

**From an AI agent (MCP):** [`schedule_article_on_substack`](/docs/build/mcp-reference/schedule_article_on_substack) needs a publish time. If the article isn't on Substack yet, it is pushed first. The agent can also set the post and email audience.

The Coach can't schedule articles.

## Unschedule

Click **Unschedule** in **Settings → Publishing**. The post goes back to being a draft on Substack. The time you picked stays in the field.

An agent can use [`unschedule_article_on_substack`](/docs/build/mcp-reference/unschedule_article_on_substack).

## Resolve a conflict

A conflict means the Substack draft was edited after your last push. WriteStack doesn't overwrite it. A dialog opens with "Substack has edits made after your last update." and a list of what changed.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep Substack version** | Pulls the Substack draft into WriteStack. Your WriteStack title, subtitle and body are replaced. |
| **Use WriteStack version** | Pushes your WriteStack version over the Substack draft. |

Edits aren't pushed on their own until you pick one.

## Video and podcast posts

A post that is a video or podcast on Substack can be edited in WriteStack, but your changes aren't pushed back. **Push** and **Schedule** are off for it. **Pull** and **Unschedule** still work.

## When the post is published

WriteStack notices when a post goes live on Substack. The article shows **Published** with the date, and edits are no longer pushed on their own. A published article stays on its date in the calendar.

## Delete or disconnect

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Delete** | Deletes the Substack draft, then the article in WriteStack. A post already published on Substack is left there and only removed from WriteStack. |
| **Disconnect from Substack** | WriteStack stops tracking the Substack draft. The draft stays on Substack. |

If the Substack draft can't be deleted (no extension, not logged in, or no access to a custom domain), the article is kept in WriteStack so nothing is lost.

Disconnecting a scheduled article doesn't cancel the schedule on Substack. Unschedule first if you don't want it to go out.

If you delete a draft on Substack, WriteStack removes its copy unless you edited it in WriteStack or it has comments. In that case it's only disconnected.

## Status for agents

An agent can read the status with [`get_article_substack_status`](/docs/build/mcp-reference/get_article_substack_status).

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `idle` | Not pushed yet. |
| `pending` | Queued. It runs the next time WriteStack is open with the extension. |
| `syncing` | An open WriteStack tab is pushing it right now. |
| `synced` | The draft (and schedule, if any) is on Substack. |
| `blocked` | The extension can't run it yet. See below. |
| `conflict` | The Substack draft has edits made after the last push. |
| `failed` | The push failed. The error text says why. |

## If it doesn't work

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Queued. It will sync when WriteStack is open with the Chrome extension." | Keep WriteStack open in a browser with the extension. It runs on its own. |
| "Install the WriteStack Chrome extension to sync." | [Install or update the extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension). |
| "Log into Substack in Chrome, then sync again." | Log into Substack in the same browser, then click **Log into Substack** or push again. |
| "Grant the extension access to your custom domain, then sync again." | Your newsletter uses a custom domain. In **Settings → Publishing**, click **Grant access**, accept the browser prompt, then push again. |
| "This draft was edited on Substack. Open it to resolve." | [Resolve the conflict](#resolve-a-conflict). |
| "Your newsletter requires a section. Open the draft on Substack, pick one under Post settings, then update again." | Pick a **Section** in [Post settings](/docs/using/articles/post-settings#audience), or on Substack. |
| "N image(s) could not be uploaded to Substack." | Push again. If it keeps failing, replace the image. |
| "Update the WriteStack extension to send audio to Substack." | Update the extension to 1.5.35 or newer, then push again. |
| "That time has already passed. Pick a later time." | Pick a time in the future. |
| "This article is already drafted on a different Substack newsletter." (agent) | Push it to that newsletter, or create a new article for the other one. |

More fixes: [Sync errors](/docs/using/troubleshooting/sync-errors).

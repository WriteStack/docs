title: Ask Quill about an article
description: How to use Quill, the Coach, inside the article editor for feedback and edits, the difference between Review and Auto mode, how to accept, skip and undo edits, and what Quill can't change.
---

Quill is [the Coach](/docs/using/concepts/the-coach) inside the article editor. It reads your draft as you write and can give feedback or edit it. In **Review** mode nothing changes until you accept each edit.

## Open Quill

- Click **Ask Quill** at the bottom right of the editor, or press Cmd+J (Ctrl+J on Windows).
- To ask about one part, select it and click the Quill icon above it. The text is attached to your message.

Quill opens on the right. It shares that space with comments, so only one is open at a time.

## Ask

Type what you want, or start with a suggestion: "Give me feedback on this draft", "Make the opening stronger" or "Suggest better titles". Quill sees your latest text, even before it saves.

## Review or Auto

Pick the mode at the top of the panel. Your choice is remembered on this device.

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Review** (default) | Quill's changes show in your article as suggestions. Nothing changes until you click **Accept** or **Skip** on each one. |
| **Auto** | Quill changes your article as it writes. You can still undo any edit from its card in the chat. |

## Accept, skip or undo

Each edit shows on a card in the chat, with **Original** and **Modified** views.

- **Accept** applies it. **Skip** leaves your text as is. **Accept all** applies every open edit.
- **Undo** takes back an applied edit.
- Every Quill edit is also in [Version history](/docs/using/articles/editor#version-history), marked **Edited with Quill**.

## What Quill can't change

An edit is not applied when:

| Note on the card | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "Quill quoted text that isn't in the draft." | The text it wanted to change isn't there. Ask again. |
| "This text appears more than once. Select the one you mean and ask again." | Select the right one, then ask. |
| "This text has changed, so the edit no longer fits." | You edited that part after Quill wrote the edit. |
| "This covers an embed, paywall or footnote, so Quill can't change it." | Edit those blocks yourself. |
| "This text has a link in it. Replacing it would drop the link." | Edit it yourself, or ask Quill to keep the link. |

## Start over

**New chat** deletes your chat with Quill for this article. The article stays as it is. Each article has its own chat.

Quill counts toward your weekly Coach usage, the same as chatting with the Coach. See [Coach limits](/docs/using/coach/limits).

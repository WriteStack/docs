---
title: Share a draft and get comments
description: How to share a private link to an unpublished WriteStack article (view or comment), what readers see, how comments, replies and resolving work, the email you get, and the limits.
---

A private link lets anyone you send it to read an article before you publish it, and optionally leave comments on parts of it. Readers don't need a WriteStack account, and they can't edit the article.

## Share a private link

1. Open the article on the **Articles** page.
2. Click **Share** in the top row of the editor.
3. In **Share a private link**, pick who can do what:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Nobody can open a link. This is the default. |
| **View** | Anyone with the link can read the draft. |
| **Comment** | Anyone with the link can read the draft and comment. They only need a name, and everyone with the link sees all comments. |

4. Click **Copy** next to the link, or **Open link** to see what readers see.

The reader always sees your latest saved version, including anything after the paywall.

Each article has one link. Switching between **View** and **Comment** keeps the same link.

## Turn a link off or reset it

- **Off** stops the link right away.
- **Reset link** makes a new link and stops the old one. Click **Reset** to confirm.
- Turning a link off and on again also makes a new link. The old one never works again.

Comments stay on the article when you turn a link off.

## When a link stops working on its own

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The article is published, or its scheduled time on Substack passes | The link closes, so the paid section stays paid. Your comments stay. |
| Your WriteStack subscription ends | Links pause. They work again when you renew. |

A reader who opens a closed link sees "This link isn't available". They don't see why.

## What readers see

- A page with the title, subtitle and body, and the banner "Private draft. Please don't forward this link."
- When you edit the draft, the page updates by itself within about 20 seconds. If the reader is in the middle of a comment, they see "The author updated this draft." with a **Refresh** button instead.
- Search engines don't index the page.

With a **View** link, readers see no comments and can't add any.

## Comments from readers

With a **Comment** link, a reader:

1. Selects text in the article and clicks **Comment**.
2. Adds their name in **Your name** the first time. Everyone with the link sees it.
3. Writes the comment and clicks **Comment**, or presses Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows).

Readers can reply to a comment and delete their own comments from the same browser. They can't resolve comments. Their comments show a **Guest** badge, and yours show **Author**.

## Comments in the editor

Click **Comments** in the editor toolbar to open the comments panel. The number shows how many threads are open. The panel shares its space with the Coach panel, so only one is open at a time.

To add your own comment, select text and click **Comment** in the menu that appears.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reply** | Replies to the thread. Replies are one level deep. |
| **Resolve** / **Reopen** | Closes a thread or opens it again. Resolved threads are under **Show resolved**. |
| **Delete** | Deletes a comment right away. Deleting the first comment of a thread deletes its replies too. |
| **Delete all from this guest** | Deletes every comment that reader left on the article. |

Highlights follow the text as you edit. If the text a comment was on is gone, the thread stays with its original quote and the note "This text has changed since the comment was left."

Comments never change the article. They aren't included in **Copy** or sent to Substack.

## Email when someone comments

You get an email when a reader comments through your link. **See comments** in the email opens the article with the comments panel open.

- At most one email every 30 minutes per article.
- Your own comments don't send an email.
- To turn them off, go to **Settings → Notifications** and turn off **Article Comments**.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Private links per account | 200 |
| Link changes (turn on, change option, reset) | 30 per hour. Turning a link off is never limited. |
| Reader comments per link | 1,000. Your own comments don't count. |
| Comment length | 2,000 characters |
| Name length | 60 characters |
| Quoted text on a comment | 500 characters |

## If it doesn't work

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Private links turn off when an article is published, so the paid section stays paid." | The article is published. You can't share it with a private link. |
| "Private links need an active subscription." | Renew your subscription. Paused links work again. |
| "You've reached the limit of private links." | Turn off links you no longer need. |
| "Too many link changes. Try again later." | You changed links more than 30 times in an hour. |
| "This link isn't available" (reader) | The link was turned off or reset, the article was published, or the subscription ended. Send the reader a new link. |
| "This link can't take more comments." (reader) | The link reached 1,000 reader comments. |
| "Too many requests. Try again in a few minutes." (reader) | Too many comments in a short time. Wait a few minutes. |
| "This part of the article can't take a comment." | That selection can't hold a comment. Select a run of text instead. |

title: Post settings
description: Every setting in an article's Post settings (the Get ready list, AI fill, thumbnail, audience, comments, tags, section, SEO title and description, URL slug, social title), what it sends to Substack, and its default.
---

**Post settings** holds the Substack settings for one article: its thumbnail, who can read it, tags, section and how it looks in search and when shared. It opens on **Get ready**, one list of everything the post needs, where AI can fill the empty settings for you. Changes save on their own and go to Substack with the next push.

## Open Post settings

1. Open the article on the **Articles** page.
2. Click **Settings** at the bottom right of the editor. **Get ready** opens.
3. To see one group of settings on its own, pick a section on the left: **Media**, **Audience**, **Discovery** or **Publishing**. Click **Done** when you're finished.

**Get ready** and the other sections show the same controls. A change in one shows in the other.

**Publishing** is where you push and schedule. See [Push an article to Substack](/docs/using/articles/substack-sync).

## Get ready

**Get ready** lists what Substack needs before the post goes out. Only the publish time is required. The rest is optional.

| Row | What it holds | Same controls as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Readers** | Audience and comments | **Audience** |
| **Thumbnail** | The cover image | **Media** |
| **Search text** | SEO title, SEO description, URL slug and social title | **Discovery** |
| **Tags and section** | Tags and section. Shows as **Tags** or **Section** if your newsletter has only one of them, and is hidden if it has neither. | **Audience** |
| **When** | The publish date, time and timezone | **Publishing** |

Each row shows its value and a state. Click a row to open its controls.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Done** | Set. |
| **Optional** | Empty. The row says what Substack does instead, for example "Without one, Substack picks an image from the post if it has any." |
| **Needs you** | This stops the post from being scheduled. |
| **Suggested** | AI filled it. Check it. |
| **Suggestion** | AI suggests a different audience. Nothing changes until you accept. |
| **Working** | AI is filling it. |
| **Didn't work** | AI couldn't fill it. Click **Try again**. |

Two things stop a post from being scheduled:

- **When** has no publish time, or the time has passed.
- The article has a paywall line but **Audience** is **Everyone**. See [Paywall line and audience](#paywall-line-and-audience).

### Schedule from Get ready

The line at the bottom says what's left, for example "One thing left: pick a publish time." or "Everything is set.".

- When something is missing, the button reads **Pick a time** or **Choose readers**. Click it to open that row.
- When nothing is missing, it reads **Schedule** and how long until the post goes out, like **Schedule in 3 hours**. It works like **Schedule** in **Publishing**. See [Schedule on Substack](/docs/using/articles/substack-sync#schedule-on-substack).

### Let AI fill the empty settings

When the thumbnail, search text, tags or section are empty, **Get ready** offers to fill them.

1. Write the article first. AI reads it to fill the settings.
2. Click **Prepare my article for release**. Rows fill in one by one. The thumbnail takes the longest.
3. Check each row marked **Suggested**:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Looks good** | Keeps the value and removes the **Suggested** mark. |
| **Edit** | Opens the row's controls. |
| **Try again** | Fills that row again. |
| **Undo** | Puts back what the row held before. |

- AI only fills settings that are empty. It never replaces something you set, and an edit you make while it runs wins.
- **Search text** fills the SEO title, SEO description and slug. **Tags and section** picks only from the tags and sections your newsletter already has.
- AI never changes **Readers** by itself. If your audience doesn't fit, it shows "AI suggests:" an audience and why, for example because the article has a paywall line, or because most of your recent posts used another audience. Click **Accept** or **Keep mine**.
- **I'll fill them in myself** hides the offer for this article. A smaller **Prepare with AI** button stays, for when you change your mind.

## Paywall line and audience

A paywall line in the article body only works when the post isn't open to everyone. Post settings keeps the two in step:

- A line under **Audience** says who reads what, for example "Paid subscribers read everything. Everyone else stops at the paywall line in your article."
- Choosing **Everyone** while the article has a paywall line removes the line from the article. Click **Put it back** to bring back the line and the audience you had.
- If the article already has a paywall line and **Everyone**, **Readers** shows **Needs you**: "This post has a paywall line but is open to everyone." Click **Make it paid** or **Remove the line**.
- Removing the paywall line in the editor doesn't change the audience.

## Media

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Thumbnail** | The cover image people see on Substack and in link previews. Click the image to upload your own. |

If the thumbnail can't be uploaded, WriteStack uses the first image in the article as the Substack cover and tells you. More on images: [Images, video and audio](/docs/using/articles/media).

## Audience

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audience** | **Everyone**, **Subscribers only**, **Paid only**, **Founding** | **Everyone** |
| **Comments** | **Everyone**, **Subscribers**, **None** | **Everyone** |
| **Tags** | Your newsletter's Substack tags | None |
| **Section** | **Newsletter default**, or one of your newsletter's sections | **Newsletter default** |

- **Subscribers only** is a freewall: free and paid subscribers can read it, everyone else can't.
- **Paid only** and **Founding** lock the post for paid or founding members.
- To lock only part of a post, use a paywall or an audience-specific section in the body. See [Formatting](/docs/using/articles/create#formatting).

### Who gets the email

When the post is scheduled, Substack emails it to:

| Audience | Email goes to |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Everyone |
| **Subscribers only** | Everyone. A freewall post still emails every subscriber. |
| **Paid only** | Paid subscribers |
| **Founding** | Founding members |

There's no setting to turn the email off.

### Tags

- Click **Add tag** and check the tags you want. Remove one with the X on its chip.
- You can only pick tags that already exist on your newsletter. Create new tags on Substack.
- When you first open a pushed article, the tags it has on Substack are loaded in.
- On push, tags you added or removed in WriteStack change on Substack. Tags someone changed on Substack since the last push are kept and copied back into WriteStack.
- Removing every tag clears them on Substack.

**Tags** only appears when your newsletter has at least one tag. **Section** only appears when it has sections. Both need the [browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) version 1.5.28 or newer. Use 1.5.35 or newer for tags: older versions can fail to read tags on drafts.

## Discovery

| Setting | What it does | If left empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEO title** | The title in search results. The counter shows 60 characters as a guide. | Substack's own |
| **SEO description** | The description in search results and the post's description. The counter shows 160 characters as a guide. | The subtitle |
| **URL slug** | The end of the post's link, after `/p/`. | The SEO title, then the title |
| **Social title** | The headline shown when your post is shared. | The post title |

- **Generate with AI** writes the SEO title, SEO description and slug from the article. It replaces what's in those fields. **Instructions** lets you tell it how to write them.
- On push, the slug is made lowercase, words are joined with hyphens, small words like "the" are dropped, and it's cut to 80 characters.
- If another post on your newsletter already uses the slug, the draft keeps its current slug and you get a warning.

## What's kept from Substack

A push doesn't change the bylines, paywall type or post type already set on the Substack draft. Change those on Substack.

## If it doesn't work

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Write or generate the article before generating SEO." | The body is empty. Write it first. |
| "SEO generation reused the article title or subtitle. Try again." | The result was the same as your title or subtitle. Click **Generate with AI** again. |
| "Add a slug before pushing to Substack." | The article has no title, SEO title or slug. Add one. |
| "Tags could not be updated on Substack." | Update the [browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) to 1.5.35 or newer, then push again. |
| "Thumbnail could not be uploaded to Substack." | Pick another image, or set the cover on Substack. |
| "Write the article first, so there is something to read." | **Prepare my article for release** needs a body. Write the article first. |
| "Couldn't write the search text.", "Couldn't pick tags. Try again." or "Couldn't make a thumbnail. Try again." | Click **Try again** on that row, or fill it yourself. |

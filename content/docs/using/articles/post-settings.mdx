title: Post settings
description: Every setting in an article's Post settings (thumbnail, audience, comments, tags, section, SEO title and description, URL slug, social title), what it sends to Substack, and its default.
---

**Post settings** holds the Substack settings for one article: its thumbnail, who can read it, tags, section and how it looks in search and when shared. Changes save on their own and go to Substack with the next push.

## Open Post settings

1. Open the article on the **Articles** page.
2. Click **Settings** at the bottom right of the editor.
3. Pick a section on the left: **Media**, **Audience**, **Discovery** or **Publishing**. Click **Done** when you're finished.

**Publishing** is where you push and schedule. See [Push an article to Substack](/docs/using/articles/substack-sync).

## Media

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Thumbnail** | The cover image people see on Substack and in link previews. Click the image to upload your own. |

If the thumbnail can't be uploaded, WriteStack uses the first image in the article as the Substack cover and tells you. More on images: [Images, video and audio](/docs/using/articles/media).

## Audience

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audience** | **Everyone**, **Subscribers only**, **Paid only**, **Founding** | **Everyone** |
| **Comments** | **Everyone**, **Subscribers**, **None** | **Everyone** |
| **Tags** | Your newsletter's Substack tags | None |
| **Section** | **Newsletter default**, or one of your newsletter's sections | **Newsletter default** |

- **Subscribers only** is a freewall: free and paid subscribers can read it, everyone else can't.
- **Paid only** and **Founding** lock the post for paid or founding members.
- To lock only part of a post, use a paywall or an audience-specific section in the body. See [Formatting](/docs/using/articles/create#formatting).

### Who gets the email

When the post is scheduled, Substack emails it to:

| Audience | Email goes to |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Everyone |
| **Subscribers only** | Everyone. A freewall post still emails every subscriber. |
| **Paid only** | Paid subscribers |
| **Founding** | Founding members |

There's no setting to turn the email off.

### Tags

- Click **Add tag** and check the tags you want. Remove one with the X on its chip.
- You can only pick tags that already exist on your newsletter. Create new tags on Substack.
- When you first open a pushed article, the tags it has on Substack are loaded in.
- On push, tags you added or removed in WriteStack change on Substack. Tags someone changed on Substack since the last push are kept and copied back into WriteStack.
- Removing every tag clears them on Substack.

**Tags** only appears when your newsletter has at least one tag. **Section** only appears when it has sections. Both need the [browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) version 1.5.28 or newer. Use 1.5.35 or newer for tags: older versions can fail to read tags on drafts.

## Discovery

| Setting | What it does | If left empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEO title** | The title in search results. The counter shows 60 characters as a guide. | Substack's own |
| **SEO description** | The description in search results and the post's description. The counter shows 160 characters as a guide. | The subtitle |
| **URL slug** | The end of the post's link, after `/p/`. | The SEO title, then the title |
| **Social title** | The headline shown when your post is shared. | The post title |

- **Generate with AI** writes the SEO title, SEO description and slug from the article. It replaces what's in those fields. **Instructions** lets you tell it how to write them.
- On push, the slug is made lowercase, words are joined with hyphens, small words like "the" are dropped, and it's cut to 80 characters.
- If another post on your newsletter already uses the slug, the draft keeps its current slug and you get a warning.

## What's kept from Substack

A push doesn't change the bylines, paywall type or post type already set on the Substack draft. Change those on Substack.

## If it doesn't work

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Write or generate the article before generating SEO." | The body is empty. Write it first. |
| "SEO generation reused the article title or subtitle. Try again." | The result was the same as your title or subtitle. Click **Generate with AI** again. |
| "Add a slug before pushing to Substack." | The article has no title, SEO title or slug. Add one. |
| "Tags could not be updated on Substack." | Update the [browser extension](/docs/using/setup/browser-extension) to 1.5.35 or newer, then push again. |
| "Thumbnail could not be uploaded to Substack." | Pick another image, or set the cover on Substack. |

---
title: Add yourself as an author
description: Add your byline to a Substack post so WriteStack can find your publication during signup.
---

WriteStack finds your publication through the posts you're credited on. If none of your posts list you as an author, onboarding shows "It seems like none of your posts include you as an author." Add your byline to at least one post, then try again.

## Steps

1. Log in to Substack and open your publication's **Dashboard**.
2. Click **Posts**.
3. Next to your latest post, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Edit**.
4. In the editor, click **Add byline** and choose yourself.
5. Click **Update** (top right), then **Update now**.
6. Go back to WriteStack onboarding and paste your profile URL again. See [Connect your Substack](/docs/using/setup/connect-substack).

Substack's editor changes from time to time. If a button looks different, look for the byline or author field at the top of the post.

## Good to know

- Published posts and drafts both work.
- You can remove the byline after signup the same way: open the post, click the **x** next to your name, and update.

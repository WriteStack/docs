---

Switch WriteStack to the dark theme from the profile menu, from search, or from Settings. Each control turns the dark theme on or off.

## Steps

1. Click your profile at the bottom of the sidebar. Click **Dark mode**. The menu also has **Settings**, **Support**, **Changelog**, and **Log out**. When the app is already dark, that item says **Light mode**, and clicking it switches back.
2. Or open search and choose **Switch to dark mode**. On a Mac the shortcut is **Cmd+K**. On Windows and Linux it is **Ctrl+K**. Alt+K does not open search. When dark mode is already on, the action says **Switch to light mode**. The same search lists a **Dark mode** row that shows Settings › Account. See [Search with Cmd+K](/docs/using/search).
3. Or open **Settings → Account**. On the **Preferences** card ("How WriteStack looks and how it shows times."), turn on the switch labeled **Dark mode**. The line under it says "Use a dark theme across the app." Turning the switch off uses the light theme. That card also has **Timezone** and **Time format**.

## If it doesn't work

- **Alt+K does nothing.** Search opens with **Cmd+K** on Mac and **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux.
- **Search does not open.** Close the note editor or any dialog first, then press the shortcut again. See [Search with Cmd+K](/docs/using/search).

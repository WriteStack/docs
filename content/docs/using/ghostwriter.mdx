---
title: Ghostwriter
description: What WriteStack's Ghostwriter mode is (writing and scheduling Substack Notes for client accounts) and how to get access to it.
---

Ghostwriter mode lets a ghostwriter manage Notes for other WriteStack users: write, schedule and generate Notes in each client's voice, from one account. Clients give the ghostwriter access to their account and can revoke it at any time.

## Who can use it

| Role | What's needed |
| --- | --- |
| Ghostwriter (writing for clients) | An **Enterprise** plan. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits). |
| Client (giving a ghostwriter access) | A WriteStack account |

Ghostwriter mode isn't listed on the pricing page. If you want to use it, [contact support](/docs/using/support) and we'll help you set it up.

## Where to find it

Both sides manage access under **Settings → Ghostwriter Access**:

- **My Ghostwriters**: ghostwriters who have access to your account. You can revoke access here.
- **My Clients**: accounts you are ghostwriting for.

---
title: Cancel your subscription
description: How to cancel WriteStack, how long you keep access, and what happens to your Notes and scheduled Notes after the subscription ends.
---

Cancel from **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices → Cancel Subscription**. You keep full access until the end of your current billing period (or the end of your free trial), and you are not charged again.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Click **Yes, cancel subscription**.
4. Pick a reason and finish. You may see an offer first (see below). To skip it, choose **Continue to cancel subscription** or **I still want to cancel**.

When it's done you see **Your subscription has been canceled**, and the billing page shows the date your subscription ends.

You can also cancel from the Stripe billing portal: click **Manage your subscription** on the same page.

## Offers when you cancel

Before you confirm, WriteStack may offer you an alternative, such as a discount, a pause, or extra trial time. Offers are optional and not guaranteed. Accept one, or continue to cancel.

## What happens next

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Until the end date | Full access. Scheduled Notes keep publishing. |
| After the end date | Scheduled Notes stop and the app locks. Your Notes stay saved. |

Your status shows **Cancels at period end** until then.

## Change your mind

Before the end date, click **Reactivate** on the cancellation notice in **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices**. Your subscription continues as before.

After it ends, subscribe again from the pricing page. There is no new free trial for returning subscribers.

## Delete your data

Cancelling keeps your data. To remove it, use **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your Notes and posts, cancels any active subscription immediately, and cancels all scheduled Notes. It can't be undone.

## Refunds

Cancelling doesn't refund the current period. See [Refunds](/docs/account/refunds).

---
title: Invoices and payment method
description: How to download WriteStack invoices and receipts, update your card, and change the billing email, name, address or tax ID.
---

Download invoices in **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices → Billing History**. To update your card or billing details, click **Manage your subscription** on the same page. It opens the Stripe billing portal.

## Download an invoice

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices**.
2. Scroll to **Billing History**.
3. Click **Download** next to the payment you need.

After each successful payment, WriteStack also emails you a payment confirmation with a **View my invoice** button.

## Update your payment method or billing details

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices**.
2. Click **Manage your subscription**. The Stripe billing portal opens.
3. In the portal you can:
   - update your card,
   - see your invoice history,
   - change billing name, email, address, phone and tax ID,
   - change or cancel your plan.

## If a payment fails

Your plan becomes past due. Open **Manage your subscription**, update your card and pay the open invoice. If the portal doesn't open, email support@writestack.io.

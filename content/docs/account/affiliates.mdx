The WriteStack ambassador program pays you **30% of every payment** from writers you refer, for as long as they stay subscribed. Joining is free and your referral link works right away.

## Ambassador program

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | 30% of every payment |
| Duration | Lifetime, as long as the customer keeps paying |
| Cost to join | $0 |
| Approval | None. Your link is live when you sign up. |
| Payout | Monthly, once your balance passes $50 |
| Payout method | PayPal or bank transfer |

### Steps

1. Go to [writestack.io/affiliates](https://writestack.io/affiliates) and click **Become an ambassador**.
2. Sign up. Your referral link is in your ambassador dashboard.
3. Share the link. The [ambassador resource kit](https://writestack.io/ambassador-guide) has logos, screenshots and a screen recording you can use.

### How referrals are counted

- The referral is tracked when someone clicks your link and credited when they subscribe, even if they subscribe later.
- Only new customers count. People who already use WriteStack don't.
- You don't need to use WriteStack yourself, though it helps.

### Rules

- No bidding on "WriteStack" or any variation of it on Google Ads, Meta or any paid platform. Commissions from branded paid search are void.
- No promoting your link through paid ads of any kind. The [Terms of Service](https://writestack.io/tos) don't allow it.
- No self-referrals. You can't use your own link to buy your own subscription.
- No pages built to rank for "WriteStack discount code" or "WriteStack coupon".
- No unsolicited DMs at scale, no comment spam, no misleading claims about results. Share real numbers only.

Breaking these rules can void commissions or close your account.

### Want a discount for your audience?

Email support@writestack.io. Custom discounts are set up for you; don't make one up.

## Coupon codes

If you have a coupon code:

- **At checkout:** enter it in the promotion code field on the Stripe checkout page.
- **From a link:** some links apply the code for you. You see **Coupon applied successfully!** on the pricing page.

If the code doesn't work, you see **Invalid coupon code**. Codes can expire or have a limited number of uses. Only one discount applies to a subscription.

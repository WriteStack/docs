## How the limits work

- **Notes queued at a time** counts Notes scheduled but not yet published. When a scheduled Note is published, you can queue another one. See [Notes and the queue](/docs/using/concepts/notes-and-the-queue).
- **Evergreen Notes** have no separate plan limit. See [Evergreen Notes](/docs/using/notes/evergreen).
- **The Coach** usage resets every Monday. Hobbyist is the 1x base; Standard gets 5 times that and Enterprise 20 times. See [Coach limits](/docs/using/coach/limits).
- **AI credits** are used by AI features such as generating Notes. They are allocated per billing period. You can buy extra credits under **Settings → Subscription → Credits & add-ons**.
- **Cross-posting** counts channels that are turned on. Turning a channel off frees its spot. See [Cross-posting](/docs/using/cross-posting).
- **Auto-DM new subscribers** sends a welcome DM when someone subscribes. The daily limit counts all your newsletters together.

## Monthly or annual

Every plan can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing costs less per month and is charged once a year. To switch, see [Change your plan](/docs/account/change-plan).

## If you hit a limit

- Upgrade under **Settings → Subscription → Billing & Invoices → Update plan**. See [Change your plan](/docs/account/change-plan).
- For queued Notes, wait for scheduled Notes to publish, or remove some from the queue.
- For cross-post channels, turn off a channel you don't use.
